How a deal works

  1. 1Send a requestA business invites a creator, or a creator pitches a brand.
  2. 2Agree on termsBarter, payment or both. Terms are fixed once both sides accept.
  3. 3Ship and createAdd a tracking number. The creator confirms delivery and sends a draft.
